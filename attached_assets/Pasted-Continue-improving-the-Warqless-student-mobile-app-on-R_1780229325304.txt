1. GOAL
   ==================================================

Create a student-facing device management experience.

The student should clearly understand:

* This account is linked to one device only.
* The current device is the registered device.
* If the student wants to change the device, they must contact support or request a device reset.
* Device reset should not happen automatically in the MVP.

This is still a Replit Free MVP, so keep it lightweight and mock/demo-based.

Do not build a heavy backend.
Do not install unnecessary packages.
Do not break the existing mobile app.
Do not break admin/publisher dashboards.
